Why Fluer exists

A house goes up only as fast as its materials arrive. On many residential sites, orders for concrete, sand, gravel and bricks are placed by phone call or text message, and nobody can say for sure where an order has reached. Information is scattered, deliveries come late, transport lets people down, and the crew waits on site for materials that may not even have left the yard.

Fluer gives builders, site managers and suppliers one shared place to see every order, so everyone knows what has been asked for, who is supplying it, when it is needed and how far it has got.

Simple on purpose

Large construction-management systems exist, but many small builders and local suppliers find them too expensive and too complicated for everyday orders. Fluer does one job well: managing and tracking routine material orders. It does not try to do everything, so it leaves out:

  • construction scheduling
  • accounting and online payments
  • a marketplace for finding new suppliers
  • vehicle tracking
  • full stock and inventory management

Who is behind Fluer

Fluer is being developed by Johannes Vadera, a Business Information Technology student at Strathmore University in Nairobi, as a practical tool for residential construction teams in Kenya.
