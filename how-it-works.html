From request to delivery

Every order on Fluer follows the same four steps, and everyone involved can see which step it has reached.

  1. Create the order. The site manager chooses the materials and quantities, picks a supplier and says when they are needed on site.
  2. The supplier confirms. The supplier receives the purchase request and accepts it, or replies if something cannot be supplied in time.
  3. Follow its progress. As the order is prepared and sent out, the supplier updates it, so nobody has to phone to ask where it is.
  4. Confirm delivery. When the materials reach the site, the delivery is confirmed and the order is closed.

What every order shows

Whoever is looking at an order, whether the site manager, the supplier or the builder, sees the same information:

  • the material and the quantity ordered
  • the supplier responsible for it
  • the date it is needed on site
  • the step it has reached, and when it was last updated
