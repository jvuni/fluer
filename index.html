Know where your materials are

Fluer is a simple web system for ordering construction materials on residential building projects in Kenya. Site managers place orders, suppliers confirm them and keep them up to date, and everyone can see how far an order has got, from the moment it is placed to the moment it arrives on site.

No more phone calls to find out whether the sand is on its way. Fluer is made for small builders and local suppliers. It runs in an ordinary web browser and covers the everyday materials, such as concrete, sand, gravel and bricks, without the cost and complexity of a large construction-management package.

Who Fluer is for

  • Site managers

    Create material orders, say when they are needed on site, and follow each one until it is delivered.

  • Suppliers

    Receive purchase requests, confirm what you can supply, and update each order as it is prepared and sent.

  • Builders

    See which materials have been ordered and when they will reach the site, so work can be planned around them.
