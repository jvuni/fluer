Materials you can order

Fluer covers the everyday raw materials that keep a residential site moving. Each one is ordered in the unit suppliers already use, so there is no confusion about how much is coming.

What to have ready

To place an order you will need:

  • the material and the quantity
  • the supplier you want to order from
  • the date the materials are needed on site
  • the site's location, so the supplier knows where to deliver
